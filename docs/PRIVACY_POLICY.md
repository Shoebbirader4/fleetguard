## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data as follows:

**Active Data:**
- Account information: Retained while your account is active
- Vehicle and maintenance data: Retained while your subscription is active
- Operational data: Retained as long as needed for service delivery

**Audit Logs:**
- Retained for 7 years minimum for legal compliance
- Includes authentication logs, data modifications, and system events

**Backup Data:**
- Daily backups: 30 days
- Weekly backups: 90 days
- Monthly backups: 1 year

**After Account Closure:**
- Most data is deleted within 30 days of account closure
- Audit logs are retained for 7 years for compliance
- Anonymized aggregate data may be retained indefinitely for analytics

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or UK, you have the following rights:

### 7.1 Right to Access
You can request a copy of your personal data we hold.

### 7.2 Right to Rectification
You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.

### 7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.

**How to Request Deletion:**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to Settings > Account > Data Management
3. Click "Request Data Deletion"
4. Confirm deletion and provide a reason
5. Note: Only Company Owners and Super Admins can delete tenant data

**Limitations:**
- Audit logs will be retained for 7 years for legal compliance
- Anonymized data may be retained for analytics
- Data necessary for legal obligations will be retained

### 7.4 Right to Data Portability
You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format (JSON).

**How to Export Your Data:**
1. Log in to your account
2. Navigate to Settings > Account > Data Management
3. Click "Export Data"
4. Select format (JSON recommended)
5. Download your data export

### 7.5 Right to Object
You can object to certain types of data processing.

### 7.6 Right to Restrict Processing
You can request temporary restriction of data processing.

### 7.7 Right to Withdraw Consent
Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

### 7.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint
You can file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**Contact Information for Data Protection Authority:**
- EU: [European Data Protection Supervisor](https://edps.europa.eu/)
- UK: [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/)
